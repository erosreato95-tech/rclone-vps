Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Michelino is used only by its owner. It makes two calendars of its own in the owner's Google accounts (the calendar.app.created scope) and can see when the owner is busy or free (the calendar.freebusy scope). It cannot read the titles or details of any other event.

It collects nothing from anyone else. Nothing is shared, sold or sent to third parties. These pages have no cookies and no analytics.

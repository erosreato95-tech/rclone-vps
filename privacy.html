Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026.

rclone-vps is used only by its owner. It can reach only the files that rclone itself creates in the owner's Google Drive (the drive.file scope), and it uses them to store encrypted backups of the owner's server.

No data from other people is collected. Nothing is shared, sold or sent to third parties. These pages have no cookies and no analytics.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

Postino is used only by its owner, on the owner's own mailboxes (the gmail.readonly scope). It reads message labels, senders and subjects, and sends the owner a private list of new messages. It does not read message bodies or attachments, and it does not change, send or delete anything.

It collects nothing from anyone else. Nothing is shared, sold or sent to third parties, and no Google user data is used to train any model. These pages have no cookies and no analytics.
